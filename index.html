    Biography

    Ahab Mullick, b. 2000

    Ahab Mullick is a writer–director based in New York. He came to cinema as a child through his immigrant father’s small, sacred DVD collection, turning a private fascination into a relentless and lifelong obsession. With no formal training or institutional support, he learned the craft by obsessively making his own work— assembling his earliest films from whatever knowledge, equipment, and collaborators he could find.

    With a rich and instinctive eye sharpened through more than a decade of tireless practice, Mullick has cultivated an unusually complete command of filmmaking while still in his twenties. Writing, directing, photographing, editing, coloring, and scoring his own work, he has developed each discipline as part of a singular cinematic language. The result is work both intimate and operatic, drawn to intimate, complex characterological examinations and to broader, incisive investigations of American life.

    Mullick’s films have appeared on SHOWstudio, Pitchfork, and BOOOOOOOMTV amongst other platforms and have been personally showcased by world-renowned photographer Nick Knight. With taste both emerging and canonical, his latest collaboration with Sage Elsesser A.K.A. Navy Blue includes the album film: Crux Ansata / Bleeding Scarlet and music videos Sir Render and Next Life. For Stone Island’s special-edition release of Sir Render, he created a companion visual piece—in tandem with photography by Liam MacRae—that premiered online and across select Stone Island stores worldwide, as part of the international launch of the album’s limited-edition vinyl. His broader commercial practice includes work with Adidas, Hugo Boss, Gucci, and other international brands.

    He is currently developing his first solo feature entitled Trying Times.
